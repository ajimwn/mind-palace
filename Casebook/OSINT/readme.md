# OSINT: The Commonplace Book

> [!quote]
> "It has long been an axiom of mine that the little things are infinitely the most important."
> — *A Case of Identity*

Holmes kept an index of everything public. OSINT is the same discipline: finding answers in open sources.

## Subjects

- Geolocation from images
- Username and social media tracing
- Reverse image search
- Domain, WHOIS, and DNS records
- Archived pages and public records

## First Moves

1. List every name, place, date, and image in the prompt.
2. Extract metadata from any provided file.
3. Pivot from each lead to a new source.

## Instruments

Google dorks, Wayback Machine, exiftool, Sherlock, Shodan, TinEye, Google Lens, Maltego
